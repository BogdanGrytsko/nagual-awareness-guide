[2022-11-27T12:03:04] About the topic of "smartphones" again - I'd like to share what I've learned and maybe someone here will find it useful.

To annihilate notifications - turn on Do Not Disturb, forever. It's the only way in settings to disable on-screen pop-up notifications. Inside the "Do Not Disturb" settings, you can configure contacts that you allow to hear the call from if you wish.
Of course, disable most apps notification service. I only have the messages, email, reminders notifications (some of them only once a day - using a setting for that in an notifications manager app called FilterBox - but that's on the side).

How to reduce the amount of your data you provide to the Techlords by 90% in 10 minutes?*

- Install and use Brave Browser. Brave search is default there, don't use Google search.
- Search and install F-Droid. This is App Store with open source (therefore safe) apps.
- Install Blokada 5 app from F-Droid. Turn on Ad Blocking in it, and activate "DuckDuckGo Tracker Radar" in Hosts Lists.
- Install and use NewPipe instead of YouTube app. That's my favourite. YT won't gather the data on what you're watching (so you won't be teaching the algorithm), no ads, and you can set up a distraction-free interface.
- Takes a bit longer: switch from Gmail to, e.g. ProtonMail. (I did this slowly and gradually, changing my email address of every website/service bit by bit, and so on)
*don't quote me on that, it's just a catchy phrase
