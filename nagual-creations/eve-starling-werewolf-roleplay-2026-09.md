woods. All before lunch." She shakes her head, looking up at the canopy of leaves as we
walk. "You're dangerous." She squeezes my arm. "I might be in trouble."

"The look on your face about the trap.” I laugh.

"Arrogant," she teases, "But you're right. I couldn't truly respect you if you just rolled over
and accepted the bond because of instinct. That would be weak. You needed to prove you
were an Alpha in your own right."

We walk a bit further when I say, “There's more…I knew right away yesterday, the moment
you talked to me. It felt like a lightning strike."

She lifts our joined hands, pressing her lips to my knuckles. "You knew yesterday? When I
was rambling about fate and moons?" She shakes her head. "You felt it. The shift in the air.
You just decided to make me work for it!" She nudges me again. "You really are a dangerous
man. I think I'm going to keep you."

"Are you hungry? I'm starving. Pizza. And milkshake."

She laughs and tugs my arm, pulling me off the path and onto the grass, turning to face me
as she walks backwards.

"A haiku, a trap, a kiss in the woods, and now a milkshake." She looks up, silver hair
catching the light through the trees. "I love it."

The forest opens up ahead, revealing the edge of town.

"Deacon?"

She stops and turns to face me fully.

"Thank you. For not running. For kissing me like I matter." She reaches up, brushing a stray
leaf from my shoulder, her touch lingering. "I know I came on strong but you met me halfway.
You didn't break. You bent, just enough to let me in." She swallows hard, "I don't know what
the rest of today holds. Or tomorrow. Or the rest of our lives. But right now, in this moment,
walking out of these woods with you...I'm happy. Bone-deep happy. And I haven't felt that in
a very long time."

She rises on her toes and presses a soft kiss to my cheek. Some crows make noises and fly
off out of a tree overhead.

"Come on! Let's go get that pizza before I turn into a hangry wolf and eat a deer."

She laughs and tugs my hand, pulling me forward toward the edge of the woods.

"Race you to the pizza place. Loser buys the milkshake."
