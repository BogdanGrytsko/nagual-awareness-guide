The Surprising Reason she Hates his Niceness

"Why does she snap at him and treat him as if he's the actual problem? Because she thinks
he's a fool. The simple reason is: She knows that she is acting like a b-word and he's being
nice to her. And in her twisted woman world any man who not only allows that treatment but
gets even nicer to the person giving it, is a fool."

I can definitely see that in me. Especially when I think of my marriage years. I then didn't
want a man who leads me but a servant who does everything for me. I remember having
been really bitchy and bossy at times, just for the sake of that fake power. I wanted to see
how far I can go and through how many hoops he'll jump. I wasn't always aware of this but
it's obvious to me now.

Why was I like that? I never learnt to respect men. My father was absent in my life, so I
never saw my mum showing respect for a companion. She was the one leading, so I have
no clue from observing during childhood what it could look like between two mature
companions. Also, when I was at my dad's place, I could basically do whatever I wanted. I
was raised like a princess there.

And what did I observe in religion which was also very dominant in my life? I received many
teachings there that a woman is the man's helper, that the man should show love for his
woman and the woman should show deep respect for her man. I could see other couples at
the congregation but I wouldn't call any one of them a role model couple. Some women were
very stubborn and basically the ones leading the marriage although to the outside that was
tried to be hidden, e.g. only men had official duties at the congregation (talks etc.), so that
created the illusion that the men were leading. But if I have a look now, in most cases it is
clear that they weren't the ones leading and that the women didn't respect their men.

Only in my early twenties did I get to know one Christian couple which I still recall as a rather
mature couple, e.g. calling each other out, but lovingly. They had also attended NVC courses
together and did regular other communication and relationship trainings. That was very
noticeable in the way they interacted with each other (I was present during some of their
discussions). And the woman always spoke respectfully of her man. And I am only clocking
this right now: She would not take herself seriously at all. She regularly made fun of herself
(and also pulled others' legs). She was never really serious unless it was really needed, e.g.
in certain deep conversations.

In any case, I am glad to have witnessed that couple. It seems to me like that was the only
one I could really have been able to look up to and take as a role model. But I only met them
in my early twenties, so all the conditioning I experienced before that happened without ANY
role models in my close circles. So I grew up without seeing how to really be with a man,
how to genuinely show respect.

And nowadays? With M., I still do not know how to genuinely show respect! I wish I could
say otherwise, but I can't! Of course, I'm not intentionally mean. But I have caught myself
several times already being bitchy. And I am sure there is still so much more immature
behaviour of mine I am not even clocking… yet.

Back to the example from the beginning: I have already clocked that I sometimes feel
frustrated and that M. was very kind to me in those situations, listening to me, holding me,
pressing his bare belly against mine (side note: That's actually one of his tricks to get me out
of my bitchy mood immediately: He calls "belly to belly", puts his naked belly against mine
and hugs me. And I can't help it but laugh and love the kindness he's bringing to me in that
moment. 🙂) However, I have also noticed that annoyance comes up when he's kind to me
when I'm annoyed. It's as if I wanted to indulge in annoyance… hold on, I am clocking right
now that I sometimes DO want to indulge in annoyance. That draws attention to me and my
princess ego craves for attention. I am seeing right now that it's a pattern of mine to indulge
in "negative" feelings which create a drama and which then draw my companion's attention
to me. What a horrible pattern!

I can also just talk to M. just like that, I don't need to create a drama. He's very
approachable and he listens with undivided attention. Why do I still create these dramas?
Lifelong training! First with my absent dad, then with my marriage mate who basically also
was absent, sucked into his gaming. But things have changed drastically to the better in my
life but I have not changed this pattern. Time to change it now! I need to bring much more
awareness to the moods I bring home and to M. I need to clock: Why am I in this mood
right now? Am I just seeking for attention from M.? Am I genuinely feeling sad or frustrated
or am I indulging in it and being a princess who wants the world to revolve around her?

The other day I had a telling conversation with M. I hadn't yet seen this video. The
conversation was about us setting our priorities during the day: When do we focus on our
successful self-employments and when do we spend time together? He said something
along the lines that he had not focused fully on his business because then he wouldn't be
able to give me attention. That's actually telling! That means either he has something to work
through in how much attention he wants to give me, or much rather: I am the one who
demands attention!!! Amazing! And I can't yet really see it. It's as if this is hidden in some
thick fog.

In the conversation with M., it was important for me to find common terms concerning the
attention. We agreed that we would define certain work hours in which we just really don't
interact with each other, also not for a kiss or a cuddle, during those hours we focus fully on
our successful self-employments. Wow. This needs to sink in. I demand attention. I demand
a lot of attention. My goodness. I have so much to learn.
