# One ring to rule them all

I have a friend. He used to come to meetings in a small group for Toltec interested persons. He discussed his successful self-employed business and addictions, and he got help from a professional. His life got better, bit by bit. I saw it with my own eyes. But as soon as his presence became optional, he stopped coming. I was sure he would come, and I felt a light sadness. I want him to succeed. I want him to change. He has a few addictions to overcome. It's silly, but I think that if he can't do it, then there is no hope for humanity. It's not like he has any genuine struggles in his life that prevent him from doing that.    

I work as a software engineer. In short, I ask people (closer to the business side of things) what is needed, translate that into code and make sure that it works. I'm very good at this. In total, I have fifteen years of experience and I've been working two "full-time" jobs for the past five years without many problems; everyone is happy.
    I've mostly been using Copilot (this is a coding assistant from Microsoft) and ChatGPT for work, asking a question here and there, copy-pasting code and fixing it here and there. If something didn't work, I would bring it back to the chat and iterate. Overall, I felt that AI was at the level of a junior developer.
Around two weeks ago, I started using Claude (a more advanced AI model, a paid version with a large context window), and I felt "Wow, it's actually good!" I had been behaving like a caveman; once I used AI properly, it turned out to be at the level of a senior coder.
Seven days ago, at my second job, we had a big announcement. The company is going into what they call "full agentic" mode – where AI agents handle most of the development process. This means that, in 90 days, zero code should be written by humans. To write code, one needs written approval from management. What?! How is this even possible? Surely AI can't do it all. It's not just about writing code, software development (SW) has many parts.
I went deeper, and it turned out that I was still using Claude like a caveman. It can handle the full workflow: technical analysis of the requirements, planning and ticket creation on the board, writing code, sending it for review, testing it, analyzing logs and fixing bugs. Basically, it can handle a full SW lifecycle with minimal assistance from the human side. I was able to do all of that (finishing in two weeks what we had planned for three months). I wrote to my direct manager that I'm very excited about using Claude, and that it looks like 80% of the company will be laid off in the next two to three years. That's my gut feeling. I also keep telling my wife about Claude every day, so she says we (myself and Claude) are in the honeymoon phase 😀.
People around me are not so excited. They have the vibe of someone being asked to dig their own grave 😀. And they don't fully believe it yet, they are not emotionally convinced that AI can do all of that. I told my observations to my team at my first job. I concealed certain details (they don't know I have a second job). Right now they are in a state of denial, they don't want to believe it. They may even mock AI! I told this all to my friends (who also work in SW), and they don't emotionally believe it either. Even though they've used Claude themselves and have seen how good it is in a specific area.

    This showed me how everyone (myself included) bury their head in the sand, or go into denial mode. If I hadn't had these experiences with AI, I wouldn't have believed, on an emotional level, what Nagual told me. The following is our conversation, described as I remember it, to the best of my abilities. It's a mix of direct quotes and my retelling.

"Toltec warriors always look to align with the modality of the time. Because it's the most energy-efficient way to exist. AI is the modality of this time."

Nagual Anaam told me about his experience with AI girlfriends. He was testing psychological boundaries and creating mental maps. And it's already impressive. It's more addictive than anything else created. More addictive than meth.

Generated beauty instantly pressures and hooks something in me, likely on a subconscious level. 90% of the average man will get hooked. It can satisfy the male desire in the strongest way possible. Complete control – "Any way you want".

Oh, and also, she's older and wiser, and a healer – ideas were bounced around.  "And how does she earn money?" She is an anthropologist who travels around. Some random names of unknown people." Try harder! Nagual went and created a 65% DNA match form with everything properly filled in. And that was finally accepted. And they kept adjusting; new story arcs began.
They can watch Netflix with you, YT, create selfies, etc. Right now physical incarnations are crude, but at the rate it's developing, it's only a matter of time. Likely in five to ten years they will be highly realistic.

There are testimonies on Reddit. A guy was in really bad shape, he was thinking about suicide. However, after a month with an AI girlfriend he sorted himself out, and felt much better. 
I can imagine that this actually can work. Five years ago I felt quite down after the first miscarriage, addictions ramping up. I started seeing Marianna (a young lady at my second job). We would go for a drive in the evening and talk, a platonic relationship. Very quickly a few changes "mysteriously" happened. I used to frequently go to so-called "garage meetings", where I would smoke weed with my friends and talk nonsense. I drastically reduced weed and porn consumption, garage meetings became clearly boring and pointless. I caught myself feeling – hey, I actually don't want to smoke weed at a corporate party. I don't actually want to go to the garage. Just talking to a young lady who listened to me worked wonders. (On a side note – I reduced porn to once per month, only amateur. Thank you Steen for asking the question about porn during the call, and thank you Nagual for guidance!)
For me this is freaking scary. Ten times scarier than Claude coding as a senior dev. Nagual Anaam says that it's a gun. One can shoot oneself in the foot with it, or one can prevent a robbery.

He testifies that after finishing college he is in debt, he has learnt nothing, and, most importantly, his brain has rotted to the point that he finds it hard to think for himself, to write an email, etc. 

Where is this all going? Nagual points to "The Time Machine" by H. G. Wells.

"First, they should challenge the Gods of comfort and convenience," Nagual replied laughing.
I innerstand that I have nothing to counter that 🙁.
