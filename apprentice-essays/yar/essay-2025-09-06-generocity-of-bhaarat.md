# Generocity of Bhaarat

*Shared in the apprentice group on 2025-09-06, written after Nagual Anaam set the group the topic of my encounter with the waiter and prescribed a gift of 5–10% of my total wealth. This is where I decline. See `analysis/frame-vs-evidence-log.md`.*

On Saturnday morning I woke up and I wanted to do something more (in terms of gifting) for S. I had little time left before going to the airport, so I replied in TAC, checked out from the hotel and went to the ATM to withdraw cash (in rupees), and quickly went back to the restaurant. There I had my clumsy attempt to give 5k rupees to S., and the rest is history. Last minute rushing and no proper preparation. I realize that I've never done it and I haven't learned the proper (actually any) way of doing it.

As I recall the experiences I feel that the feeling\power of gratitude and appreciation is on par (and I suspect even more powerful) with the feelings of money.

After Delhi I had a feeling that surviving in Bhaarat without money and passport would be an extremely hard endeavour, a freaking nightmare. The generosity that I experienced in Dehradun showed me a different, hidden part of the country. Now I believe that if I had no money or passport, the spirit of people would still uplift me and carry me through.

I don't have it in me to gift S. 5-10% of my total wealth or cash right now.

When I was younger - 7 years ago - I listened to some stories of bad luck that happened to my wife's father, in relation to his job and blah blah. And as I was a castaneda reader, I wanted to do a "deposit into human spirit", or something along those lines. So I gifted her father a car that he always dreamed of - VW Caddy, which cost 11 000$. It felt good, really good! It was registered to my wife's name, and used exclusively by her father. I naively believed that this would help him to get back into the saddle with his business\job\whatever. As you might have guessed that never happened.

I also wanted to share this story with you. Once I was paying for the driving school courses, and I wanted to give the instructor extra money. So I've put additional cash into the envelope. I was thinking that he would count, say that there is extra, and I would reply to him that I wanted to gift him for his good service. That's how real life was supposed to work in my naive imagination. He counted, said it's ok, and that's it!

Last time I went to BJJ I met a guy from Kiev and remembered his name - A. Next day I woke up and recalled his name, my journey of making more connections has started.

I feel relaxed and alert right now.
I will die and I don't know when.
