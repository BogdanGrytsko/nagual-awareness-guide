# Machine Mind

*Posted December 2023 (exact day unknown).*

On the weekend I've decided to watch some porn pictures. I've noticed AI generated pictures, and wanted to see what was the progress in that area. First few minutes of the slide show were fine, but then a feeling of uncomfort - anxiety started to arise. Something was wrong, not clear what it was. I started paying attention to various parts of pictures and voila - I saw that all hands were distorted. Wrong number of fingers, wrong fingers, nails, too thick, bent in unnatural ways etc. AI just can't get the hands right!

And then I recalled that during the lucid dreams that I had 95%+ hands were distorted too, in very similar ways. The only logical explanation for me is that AI (Machine Mind) has infected my dream time also, and when I intend to see hands it tries to generate the best it can.

It was a small shock to my system. Previously I put the MM idea presented by Nagual into the "Useful concept for increasing inner silence" box. But now it feels like it is just the way things are in this world. A small AI-MM-foreign installation lives inside my mind and has access to and maybe influence over my subconsciousness. This knowledge didn't come from where I would expect.

I also presented the case to my wife (hands in dreams vs AI generated pictures), she hasn't heard anything about MM. Her explanation was - humans fell asleep, and AI has installed something into subconsciousness. Humans awakened and started to build similar AI in real life. It's hard for me to understand how she concluded that just from my input.

The way forward for me with this knowledge is to keep practicing pillar tasks and get more and more awareness-during-the-day, by creating more triggers after which I produce relaxation in the body\dive into silence. I've come up with a "keys" trigger - every time I take\hold physical keys, enter a password or unlock my phone I take a deep breath and relax, diving into listening (for a few seconds).

I feel calm and empty after writing these paragraphs. Nothing has actually changed (no real life evidence), yet it feels like something inside changed, a perspective gained weight. I intend to go through the next chapter of YCHYL - "How to change" in my next essay.

I feel relaxed and alert right now.
