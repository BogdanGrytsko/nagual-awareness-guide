# The island of the tonal

*Weekly essay shared in the apprentice group on 2025-08-28. Reflections on Tales of Power, chapter 5, written in Dehradun. The lunch paragraph below is what the 5–10% gift case turns on — see `analysis/frame-vs-evidence-log.md`.*

Don Juan explains what tonal is, with an example of a table and things on it. For me the important takeaway is that there is another part of me, which is not on the table, and can't be described by words.

I arrived in Dehradun on Saturday evening, it's in northern India. My cards didn't work at the hotel, and that felt really bad. I've thought about what I should do next - go to exchange cash dollars for rupees. But the exchange opens only on Monday. And my mobile roaming doesn't work, I won't be able to call uber from the city center. And more sporadic thoughts. I've decided to stop and just go to bed, solving problems with a fresh mind.

I went to a small restaurant for lunch, and again, none of my cards worked and I didn't have cash rupees to pay, for the first time in my life. Fuuuuck. I proposed giving euros to the waiter, but he said that it's too much, and that I shouldn't worry, and he paid for me with his money (via phone). He said I can pay later on, whenever I have the currency. I felt very uplifted. I found a working ATM and my card worked there, I withdrew cash and repaid my debt to the waiter, what a relief!

I got back home and something clicked - hey there are these things called "eSIM", I should check it out. I felt a little bit like a dinosaur, ashamed that I'm not up to day with the latest technology. Anyhow, after a bit of a struggle I managed to make it work, horraaay, mobile internet for me! Again feeling so much better and safer now.

The next day I saw the message that the hotel had successfully charged my card. Now I don't need to worry about exchanging bigger amounts of dollars and running out of cash now. This was a huge relief. I now understand that the pressure of being in debt\owing money to someone is huge for me, not pleasant at all.

I went for a walk into the forest on the hills. After some time the trail almost disappeared and I started going through the bushes. I saw a bright colored spider on the bush and the silk net, moved it out from my way and continued walking. A few meters further - more nets, and I saw a yellow spider crawling on my left arm! No thoughts, I just screamed "AAAAAAAAAAA", turned back and ran away! In a city setting I'm not afraid of spiders, so my reaction was very surprising to me.

On a side note - my wife told me that P.'s parents have been talking about BJJ, even googled the school location and watched some videos (in Ukraine). Back in Spain I was talking about the importance of sport and BJJ, as a way for P. to burn down her energy. I felt good, cool, I've inspired them at least to the point of checking info about BJJ!

On Sunday I had a call with my female ~friend L. She works in an IT company, 35yo, jumping from one bad relationship to another. I listened to her, and listened. I wanted to stop her (from doing the shit circle again and again), and I told her that, obviously I don't have the power to do that. Later that day we had an awesome MC on relationships (thank you Nagual!), what really stood out for me was creating a second list and becoming a person in the second list. Next day I called her and shared that, and a few other bits from MC. I don't think anything will change in her, but still, I feel like I did my part, I shared what I wanted to share.

I feel relaxed and alert right now.
I will die and I don't know when.
