Very Effective Gratitude Meditation

Nagual Anaam teaches the meditation:

*Get into a relaxed and alert state.*
*Bring into your mind something that you know is always giving to you. For someone that might be a phone that they had for years. Or your car. Or your gaming console. It gives to me every day. I get so much from it. Keep it to the thing for now, rather than a person. And just bring a sense of appreciation to that. And allow that sense of appreciation to expand in your body. Feel the bodily sensation that for you stands as deep appreciation. In your mind's eye say thank you to that object. It is your power object. It is your shamanic talisman. It is the one thing that you interact with daily, and it keeps giving to you. Keeps giving you energy.*
*From here you allow your awareness to expand to something else.*

I started doing this meditation towards my keyboard, mouse, PON and boxing gloves. I noticed that it's quite hard for me to visualize them, and to keep them in my mind's eye, even one object at a time. It's a bit easier when I associate a sound with an object – like mouse clicks. Overall I feel it's an efficient practice.

When I was a kid I had a Canadian grandma. She sent us 1000 CAD every year (while my father was alive). In 2020 I realised that I had never wholeheartedly thanked her for that. I wrote an email, but it was too little too late – she never read it, as she died soon after. She owned a house and left a testament. And two weeks ago I got an email from lawyers saying that most of the legal procedures are done and everyone will be getting this and that. My share of the pie is a nice 3.3% which amounts to around 12,500 dollars. Thank you thank you thank you! Of course it will take time to receive them, could be a year or two.

And then I received an email in Ukrainian, supposedly from my paternal half-brother D. It was written with mistakes. He claimed that there was another testament, and he knew where it was, and according to which the inheritance distribution should be different – I should be getting 10%. He wanted to connect via TOR and what not, because gmail is spied upon and is not secure. D. left Ukraine a long time ago and wasn't in contact with me for 15+ years. Supposedly he went to Russia to become a teacher in school, who knows. Mentally he was quite bad 15 years ago – he was trying to run away and hide from everyone, as he was convinced that people wanted to poison him. Including me, he wouldn't eat at my home, not even oranges that I cut in front of him and ate.

So even before reading the email I had a feeling that it was a scam, 95%. After reading I became convinced it was. I noticed a few things:

* The email started with "I got your email address from a document XXX". I checked the documents, but the email addresses of beneficiaries are not present there.
* He claims there is a second testament – why not just send it to lawyers directly? Like what's the fuss with TOR etc.
* "secret testament" would reduce his share from 13.3% to 10%. How kind of him to sacrifice his share so that I (and others) get more!
* My brother T. didn't receive such an email. This was doubly confirmed – I received an email again, in bad English this time, while T. didn't.

So this leads me to think that scammers are targeting me. Likely the primary goal is identity theft – trying to gain access to everything I have access to.
I knew that "D." started to contact his mother last year. Calls on the phone, never video. So I showed the email to her and asked – did he write it? "Yes yes, for sure, he contacted me from this address. And he always writes with mistakes". Riiiight. I brought my concerns to her, to which she had no answers. "Oh so what, but does he ask you to do something illegal? It's joining chat" was her reply. She also claimed that she is not stupid and she is not blinded by a mother's love. She claimed that he didn't know where the testament was, so I had to send her a screenshot in which I underlined with a huge red line his statement – that he knows. More questioning revealed that he couldn't create an email account (I was laughing hysterically inside when I heard it) so he asked her to do it, and to install certain things. To me it was clear that her accounts were fully hacked and under the control of scammers.
I told her what I thought – likely D. was already dead. By her own account, he was brought to the KGB for questioning (once the war started), so obviously he told everything. And the capabilities of AI to generate text and voice that impersonates someone are incredible. She said AI is shit, and her mother's heart knows it's him. I asked if she had ever used AI – of course she hadn't. We did talk a bit more; she expressed some old resentments and I left it there. I warned T. about the situation, and let him know that I thought that her accounts were fully compromised.
I feel grateful for the apprenticeship training – it allows me to approach such matters with a clear head and to integrate my gut feelings about things.
