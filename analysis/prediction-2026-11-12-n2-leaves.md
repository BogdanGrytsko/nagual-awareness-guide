1. He is removed from TAC.

the silence.

**Scoring:** record the exit date, or "still active" at each checkpoint. P1 is a calibration
entry, not a hit or miss. It is scored with the Brier score: (probability − outcome)², where the
outcome is 1 if he has left by that date and 0 if not. It is pooled with `yar`'s other
probability predictions over time.

## P2: if he leaves, the explanation will point at him

> If N2 leaves, the explanation offered in TAC will locate the cause in him: his commitment,
> mindset, wanting, self-sabotage, victim stance, or a "break" he chose. **Neither the fees nor
> the group's treatment of him will be named as a cause.**

**Scoring window:** TAC messages from his exit date to 30 days after it, by the teacher or by
apprentices. Add any call `yar` was on, if it's discussed there.
- **Hit:** at least one attribution to him, and no message naming money, fees, penalties or call
  costs, or the group's treatment of him (the 2026-11-11 pile-on, "hunts", tone) as a cause of
  the exit.
- **Miss:** anyone with standing (the teacher, or a long-standing apprentice without pushback)
  names money or the group's treatment as a cause. That includes a plain *"he couldn't afford
  it"*, as was said of `mira`.
- **Unclear:** no explanation is offered at all. That happened with `kesh`, whose exit drew no
  comment from the teacher. Silence is recorded but not scored as a hit.
- **Not scored:** P2 does not apply if he hasn't left by 2027-05-12.

**Why P2 matters more than P1:** `why-apprentices-leave.md` documents that exits are explained by
attributes of the person or by unobservables, never by the fee structure or the group. A day
before this was sealed, an apprentice said it in advance to N2 himself: *"not having enough money
for the apprenticeship is a sign of not wanting it seriously enough"* (#36562). P2 can genuinely
fail, and a miss would be evidence against a pattern this project has documented. Score it
straight.
