# Scored outcomes — 2021–22 pilot, first pass

Companion to `commitments-2021-22-frozen.md`, which was committed to git **before** any
outcome in this file was consulted. Nothing in the frozen register was added, removed or
edited to produce these scores. Buckets are the pre-registered five: `kept` · `partly` ·
`abandoned` · `reframed` · `unknown`.

**Status: 37 of 115 scored, and one reclassified out of the register entirely.** These are the ones where the archive gives a real answer —
overwhelmingly the Tier A commitments, which is the point of tiering. The remaining 77 are
mostly Tier C single self-reports whose outcome is a matter of the person's own later
say-so; they need the second independent pass that rule 5 requires, and they are listed as
`unscored` rather than guessed at.

Evidence is cited as `c<id>` / `e<id>` — the Telegram message id, retrievable from
`tg-private/tac-full.jsonl` and `tg-private/docs/`.

## One entry reclassified, not scored

**C047 (`hale`, "1 I quit smoking", 19 Jan 2022) is not a commitment and should not have
been in the register.** Read in full it is a *life-evidence list* of things already
achieved: "1 I quit smoking 2 moved to a new location 3 changed jobs 4 got divorce filed
ending a 30 year marriage 5 dissolved 4 bands I was playing in" (`c8112`). That is an
outcome report, not a forward promise, and the prefilter caught it because "I quit" is in
the pattern list. Scoring it as a kept commitment would have inflated the keep rate with
something that was never a commitment at all.

It matters for a different reason, and belongs in `why-apprentices-leave.md`: **three
months before he was removed, `hale` reported five simultaneous Tier A life changes** — an
addiction ended, a relocation, a job change, a 30-year marriage dissolved. He then left
without notice, one month after an "escalated impeccability" ultimatum. Any account of his
departure that treats him as someone who was not doing the work has to get past that
message first.

Also corrected: **C013 (`dov`, cut out sugars and start keto)** was scored `kept` in the
first draft of this file on no evidence whatsoever. There is exactly one later
diet-related message from him in the following eleven months, and it is a comment on
somebody else's essay. It is `unknown`.

## Reading against the pre-committed interpretation

The design file fixed the interpretation in advance. Holding to it:

- **"High keep-rate at Tiers A/B → the apprenticeship produces real change."** Partially
  met. 11 kept of 37 scored is not high, but the kept ones are concentrated exactly where
  the evidence is hardest — coffee quits with withdrawal detail, a teaching certification
  converted into taught classes, a country move, competitions with video. When these people
  did keep a commitment, they kept a real one.
- **"Low keep-rate with heavy Tier D volume → language substitutes for change."** Not
  supported in this window. Tier D is 6 of 115 in the frozen register, and `reframed` did
  not fire at all. The failure mode here is **silence**, not substitution.
- **The A:B diagnostic.** Unanswerable as designed, and the reason matters: only 4 of 115
  written commitments are teacher-assigned, because assignment happens on the paid calls
  and no call is in the archive. What can be said is narrower and more surprising —
  **of the three teacher commitments that are checkable, two were abandoned outright**
  (C014, C082) **and both were promises made to the whole group.** The apprentices' keep
  rate in this sample is better than the teacher's.

## What this pass does not establish

- **One pass, not two.** Rule 5 requires a second, differently-framed pass with
  disagreements read by hand rather than averaged. This is pass one.
- **The recall check is still outstanding.** The low band and rejected candidates were
  never hand-read, and one commitment already surfaced by accident during scoring that the
  prefilter had missed — `arlo`'s "I'll have my EFT Level 1 & 2 completed by 22 November"
  `c13168`, made the same day as C094 and kept. So the register is a floor, and the true
  denominator is larger than 115.
- **`hale` is under-represented** for the reason given in the frozen file: 16 of his 18
  essays in this window are image-only scans. Seven were transcribed by hand on 2026-08-17
  and none contained a dated commitment — his writing is overwhelmingly book exegesis with
  very little first-person action in it. That is itself a datum about him, but it is based
  on 7 of 16.
- **Nothing here speaks to the falsifier** in `frame-vs-evidence-log.md`. A system can
  make people act and still never revise itself. These are separate questions and this file
  only answers the second one.
