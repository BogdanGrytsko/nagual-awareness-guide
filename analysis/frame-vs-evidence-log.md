# Frame vs evidence — a running log

A place to accumulate concrete cases where a teaching met contradicting evidence from an apprentice's own life, and to record **what got revised: the apprentice, or the frame.**

This is a data-gathering exercise, not a verdict. The point is to replace recollection with dated citations, because memory conforms to whatever frame you currently hold — which is the very thing under examination.

## The question

> Show one case where an apprentice ran a teaching, reported that it didn't work, and **the frame — not the apprentice — was what got revised.**

There are two further standing questions, both pre-registered before any scoring:

- `commitments-vs-outcomes.md` — **does the frame produce change, or mainly language about change?**
- `sse-outcomes.md` — **what did five years of pushing successful self-employment actually produce?** The one prescribed outcome that leaves externally checkable evidence, because it is denominated in money.

This file asks whether the teaching can be corrected; those two ask whether it works. All three can disagree, and that is fine — a system can be poor at correcting itself and still be good at making people act.

Two or three such cases would settle it. As of 2026-08-15, none has been found; the search has been by recollection only (three years of membership), which is weak evidence either way. Essay and Telegram archives are now searchable, so this should be settled with citations.

## Why it matters

A group where the frame always wins loses the ability to learn. Every failure is absorbed as a failure of the member — insufficient application, insufficient wanting, forgetting — so no observation can ever reach the teaching. Note that this is not a claim about anyone's sincerity. It is a claim about a structure, and structures do this regardless of the good faith of the people inside them.

## Entry format

```
### <short title>
- **Date(s):** when the evidence appeared / when the frame reasserted
- **Source:** file or chat + date, so it can be re-read
- **Teaching:** the claim as stated
- **Contradicting evidence:** what the apprentice actually observed
- **What happened next:** who or what got revised
- **Verdict:** frame won | frame revised | advice adapted | open
```

### Unconditional love — his own mother

- **Dates:** evidence 2025-12-25, frame reasserted 2026-04-21
- **Source:** `apprentice-essays/holt/nagual-telegram-2025-12-25-reflections-on-sexual-insecurity-part-2.md`, then `apprentice-essays/holt/nagual-call-2026-04-21-the-mating-instinct-game.md`
- **Teaching:** no female has ever loved a male, mothers included; what looks like motherly love is hormones and the mating instinct.
- **Contradicting evidence:** holt works through his own grudge and concludes the opposite, naming the conflict as he does it: *"I'll use the term unconditional love for now, although Nagual Anaam has made it clear in the past that it's all mating instinct and there is not unconditional love… But then I realized how much she loves me, and how unconditionally she loves me."* His evidence: she believed he was in a cult, could not understand his choices, and still read Eckhart Tolle and Louise Hay to try to understand him. *"If that's not proof enough for her motherly love… how could I have asked for more?"* He reports it worked — *"This realisation relaxed something in me."*
- **What happened next:** four months later he restates the teaching unchanged, including *"that 'unconditional motherly love' is also just hormones and the mating instinct."* The lived conclusion is gone, without being argued against.
- **Verdict:** **frame won.** He noticed the contradiction, held it briefly, and it was overwritten rather than resolved.

### "This apprenticeship is not helping me to heal" — the stated reason overwritten

- **Dates:** evidence 2022-05-05, frame reasserted 2022-05-12
- **Source:** TAC, ids 10341–10381 (his farewell and his reason) and 10515–10534 (the post-mortem). Case detail in `why-apprentices-leave.md`.
- **Teaching:** the apprenticeship produces healing and wellbeing, alongside personal power; physical conditions are approached through the training.
- **Contradicting evidence:** after eighteen months, `arlo` quit and explained why, unprompted, in the group: *"I have not given up on becoming a warrior. It's just that over the past few months my health conditions have become worse, and every-time I have a flare up of pain, it sets me back so much and it's very demotivating. I've had so many setbacks because of this. **I just don't feel this apprenticeship is helping me to heal at this time.**"* A clean report of a teaching not producing its result, from a long-standing member, with no hostility in it.
- **What happened next:** eight days later an apprentice asked what could be learned from his leaving. The answer named the things he had thanked the teacher for and asked the group *"What would you thank me for if you were to leave?"*, concluding: *"Those who have this to thank the nagual for, they gain - those who are focused on everything but this will leave. **This is why `arlo` left.**"* His stated reason was not addressed, contradicted, or mentioned. Nobody in the group raised it either, and it was visible to all of them.
- **The rule that made it possible, stated ten months earlier** (2021-07-03): *"It's a great idea to really study why people leave apprenticeship. It will show you how almost all the time the reasons are not what they seem… The machine mind is the ultimate spin doctor."* A standing instruction that a leaver's stated reason is not the real reason — which is what converts a study of departures into an exercise that no stated reason can ever inform. See `why-apprentices-leave.md`.
- **Verdict:** **frame won**, and this is the strongest instance in the log so far, because the contradicting evidence was explicit, public, recent and unrefuted — it was simply replaced with a defect of the apprentice. Note the asymmetry: had his health improved, the archive shows that would have been logged as life evidence for the training.
- **Also true:** he returned four months later, so he did not experience it as a rupture; and the teacher's claim that `arlo` credited him with real benefits is corroborated by `arlo`'s own farewell, which thanks him warmly. The problem is not that the gratitude was invented. It is that gratitude was substituted for the reason.

### The 5–10% gift — S. in Bhaarat

- **Dates:** event 2025-08-28; escalation 2025-08-30 to 2025-09-13; canonised in the group's retelling 2026-04-06; retold in an undated Sunday masterclass
- **Source:** TAC, message ids 30228–30532 (thread pulled to `tg-private/thread-gift.txt`); the six essays it produced in `tg-private/docs/`; the prior teaching at TAC ids 17657–17658 (2023-09-08) and the gifting-task instructions relayed at id 22725 (2024-05-13)
- **Teaching:** the gifting task — give to one person who has benefited you, as an act of power rather than charity. On the amount, asked point-blank *"What amount should I go for?"*: **"Only what you feel to give. There's no set amount — gift whatever you feel comfortable with. The amount won't be the important thing… it's the gesture that's of value."** Practice across the group, before and after this episode: €50–150, vouchers, a plant, a set of tools.
- **What actually happened:** a waiter, S., paid for Yar's lunch from his own pocket when Yar's cards failed in Dehradun and trusted him to return. Yar repaid the same day, tipped 20%+, became a daily customer for a week, then went back specifically to gift: **€50 refused, ₹5,000 refused**, and later an Amazon India gift card that his cards would not process from Spain. Four attempts, two declined by S. himself. S. then sought him out and gave him pastries and called him a friend.
- **The escalation:** in one message the teacher calls this *"the highest quality exchange between two beings that can be possible"* and *"the bare minimum"* produced by *"fear & self obsession"*, and prescribes **"5-10% of your total wealth or cash (sneaked in a test there 😊)"** — on Yar's net worth at the time, roughly **two to three orders of magnitude above the group's practiced standard**, and around four orders above the sum S. fronted. Another apprentice (rook) bid the figure up as a "bold" suggestion and, when both Yar and a third apprentice asked him to state it concretely, never did. The teacher: *"this is what I would explore too however I doubt [`yar`] has that in him."* (name redacted in the quote)
- **Contradicting evidence Yar gave:** he declined in writing — *"I don't have it in me to gift S. 5-10% of my total wealth or cash right now"* — citing an $11,000 car he had gifted his father-in-law years earlier, which changed nothing, and extra cash to a driving instructor that was silently pocketed.
- **What happened next:**
  1. **Nobody asked whether the instruction was consistent with "there's no set amount."** The inconsistency was never noticed, in either direction.
  2. Five apprentices were assigned essays on the episode. All five are confessions of the author's own defect — self-obsession, white man's mentality, princess, entitlement. None examines the proportionality of the prescription. One (rook) notes his central point probably doesn't apply to Yar and keeps it anyway.
  3. **holt's essay is the clearest case of self-revision to fit the frame:** he registers that 5–10% of total wealth *"would be way too much"*, relabels that reaction as *"the fear of giving"*, and concludes it *"absolutely feel[s] like an adequate gift."* His own subsequent gifts stayed at tools-for-workmates and a €150 dinner. Verbal assent, unchanged behaviour, gap unnoticed by anyone.
  4. Yar was pressed by proxy — the teacher instructing holt to *"nail him to the wall"*, adding *"he has learnt nothing and he won't change… this is only for you to learn how to play with the average man and practice stalking."* Yar held: *"Right now I don't want to change that… I like the way things are."*
  5. **Then he wrote the assigned essay and converted his own refusal into a character flaw** — self-importance, needing to be right, *"the ultimate consequence would be me becoming an extremely toxic, self-righteous person… As good as dead."* His life evidence was never engaged by anyone, including himself, after that.
  6. He named the sealing mechanism in the group and it drew no response: **"No, if I keep bringing it again and again my benefiting from apprenticeship will plummet to 0."**
  7. **The frame absorbed the episode as confirmation.** By 2026-04-06 it is repeated as *"Yar was offered a chance to be generous to waiter that could have unlocked doors of abundance for him"* — false on the facts — and in that same thread **Yar applies the identical logic to holt**, arguing he should have given more. Per Yar's recollection, a Sunday masterclass version has the teacher saying he had himself **closed "that gate"** for Yar, hinting at abundance withheld.
- **Verdict:** **frame won on the interpretation; the apprentice held on the action.** Not a falsifier. The teaching was never revised — its own prior form ("no set amount") was overridden without anyone registering a contradiction, the demand never became anyone's practice, and non-compliance was reclassified first as a character defect and then as a supernatural penalty, which is unfalsifiable in both directions.
- **Also true, and against the easy reading:** the teacher's question is what sent Yar back to the restaurant a third time, which produced the pastry exchange that Yar rates as the high point of the trip — that cost nothing and would not have happened otherwise. And Yar's counter-evidence is weak by the teaching's own distinction: the $11,000 car came with an expected outcome attached, so it tests investment, not gifting. Nobody in the group made that argument either.

### The Africa exchange — a factual dispute answered with a credential challenge

- **Dates:** 2022-12-13 → 2022-12-15. `arlo`'s last message: 2022-12-30.
- **Source:** TAC chat `c13654`–`c13705`. Found 2026-08-17 by the per-person dossier method,
  not by any of the four vector studies — none of which could see it.
- **Teaching:** that a rejection read as racial is the reader's own *"blame game"* and
  *"false hierarchical worse-than/better-than comparative game"*; and factually, that small
  companies face serious administrative obstacles hiring outside the EU.
- **Contradicting evidence:** `mira`, who is Kenyan, was rejected from a freelance job on the
  stated ground that the company *"prefer people from Europe"*. Her first reading was *"a ploy
  to get rid of me / considered less than / racism."* She then abandoned it, and named the
  lever: *"This change of perspective came after the nagual shared his opinion with me and said
  **he doesn't hire people from 'third world countries' due to a grovelling mentality**."*
  `arlo` disputed the reversal on the merits — the tax and admin reasons were *"laughable"*, he
  too had read the rejection as possibly racist, and the recruiter had said *Europe*, not *EU*,
  which are different things.
- **What happened next:** the teacher answered the substance with *"There are serious admin
  hassles for small companies employing anyone outside the EU these days especially from **a
  fuck all place like Africa**"*, and answered `arlo`'s Europe/EU distinction with a challenge
  to his standing rather than to his reasoning: *"I'm assuming you know because you have
  personal life evidence to back it up? When was the last time you traveled to Europe? 😊"*
  Another apprentice then turned on `arlo` over a misreading of his position, which `arlo`
  corrected. His closing line: *"The other stuff about the recruiter was not that important,
  but for some reason that's what the focus has become."* **He posted for the last time fifteen
  days later and left without a farewell.** Nine months after this, `mira` was removed for
  inability to pay.
- **Verdict:** **frame won.** The disputed claim was never engaged; the disputant's
  entitlement to speak was. Two revisions occurred and neither was to the frame: `mira`
  revised her own perception into a defect, and `arlo` left.

### The pre-decided healing that failed on schedule

- **Dates:** commitment 2021-09-10, reported failure 2021-12-31.
- **Source:** `c5700`-era chat and essay `e7590`.
- **Teaching:** pre-decide a date by which a condition will be released, then act accordingly
  — the technique as taught, and used by `arlo` on two conditions at once.
- **Contradicting evidence:** on the date he set, in writing: *"I'd pre-decided to have healed
  myself of this condition by 31 December 2021, but it does not appear that this is going to
  happen… **I'm not sure how to proceed at this point being that the healing modalities I've
  worked with have not helped.** I feel I've done as much internal energy work as I possibly
  can (the Louise Hay work, the healing meditations, the EFT, the Theta healing, the
  acupuncture, etc.). And so, where do I go from here?"*
- **What happened next:** no revision, and no answer to the question he asked. He was reminded
  — via don Juan's diarrhea analogy, which he recounts himself — that he *"concerns himself too
  much about petty things"*. He continued, adding a urologist and a five-day fast.
- **Verdict:** **frame won — but log the honest complication.** The *same* technique applied to
  his knee on a parallel date **worked**, and he reported that too, three months early. So this
  is not a method that never works; it is a method whose failures produce no adjustment while
  its successes are credited. That asymmetry is the finding, not the failure.

## State of the investigation (2026-08-15, end of day)

**What exists now.** Read-only Telegram access via `scripts/tg.py` and `scripts/tg_docs.py` (credentials in `~/.tg`, outside the repo). The **full apprentice-group history is exported** — 34,609 messages, 1.31M words, Oct 2020 to Aug 2026 — plus an index of all **2,078 attachments**, of which 1,719 are essays. Both live in `tg-private/`, gitignored because they contain other members' private material, and both are re-creatable from the scripts. Four of Yar's own essays are converted into `apprentice-essays/yar/`; a handful of others' essays have been pulled where a case needed them.

**Four analysis threads, all with their rules fixed before scoring:**
- this log — can the teaching be corrected?
- `commitments-vs-outcomes.md` — does the frame produce change, or language about change?
- `sse-outcomes.md` — what did six years of prescribing self-employment produce, in money?
- `why-apprentices-leave.md` — four departures reconstructed in full, plus the mechanism.
- `health-outcomes.md` — who got ill, who got better, and how each outcome was explained.

**What is known.** Three cases of the frame overriding lived evidence are logged above, the strongest being an apprentice's own stated reason for leaving being replaced by "insufficient gratitude" eight days later. Two cases of advice genuinely adapting are logged as counter-examples, on purpose. On departures: nine exits carry nine explanations, every one a property of the person who left; a penalty or ultimatum precedes exit within 1–10 weeks in seven traceable cases; essay-submission rates are roughly flat across the group while enforcement is not, so what varies is who gets asked; and leavers are cleared out of adjacent groups within days, which is why no member can check any of it from inside.

**What is not known, and matters.** This rests almost entirely on **written** material. Private calls are where most of the teaching happens and none are in the archive; several essays are an apprentice's *summary* of a call, which is a filtered source; masterclasses are invisible, including the one where the teacher is said to have told the group he had closed a "gate" for Yar. The 1,719 essays are indexed but **not yet converted to text**, so every financial and commitment finding here is provisional until they are. Yar has recordings of his own calls and intends to bring them in.

**The standard to keep.** The falsifier at the top of this file is still unanswered in the direction that would vindicate the group: no case has been found where an apprentice reported a teaching not working and the *teaching* was what changed. That remains the thing to look for, and evidence for it counts exactly as much as evidence against — which is why the counter-examples above stay in, and why the day's own errors are corrected in place rather than quietly dropped.
