# Frozen commitment register — 2021–22 pilot

**This file is the pre-registration.** It was written and committed to git on 2026-08-17 **before any outcome was looked at**, which is rule 1 of `commitments-vs-outcomes.md`. There is deliberately no outcome column. Scoring happens in a later commit, against this list, with nothing added or removed.

**115 commitments**, 81 of them carrying a date or cadence that makes absence visible. Window 2021-01-01 to 2022-12-31 — decision D1, taken 2026-08-17.

## How this list was built

1. `scripts/extract_commitments.py` scanned the whole window — 563 essays (519,079 words, 18 of them image-only and therefore empty) and 12,183 chat messages (435,920 words) — for first-person commitment language, and produced 2,051 candidate sentences.
2. `scripts/rank_commitments.py` scored each candidate on volition, cadence, deadline, quantity and concreteness, minus quotation, hypothetical and non-first-person markers. 388 landed in the high and mid bands.
3. Every one of those 388 was read by hand. The ones below are those with an action specific enough that its absence would be visible — the definition in the design file. The rest were dropped as reflective prose, book exegesis or conversational filler, not commitments.

**Known gap, stated before scoring:** the 975 low-band and 688 rejected candidates have not been hand-read. The recall check the design file requires — hand-reading one full month end to end and counting what the prefilter missed — has NOT been done yet. Until it is, this list is a floor on the number of commitments in the window, not a census.

**Second known gap:** `hale` wrote 18 essays in this window and 16 of them are image-only scans with no text layer (`tg-private/unreadable.txt`). So his commitments are represented by chat only, and he is under-counted here by construction. Do not read his low count as reticence.

**How that gap gets closed without breaking the freeze.** `hale`'s scans are being transcribed by hand as of 2026-08-17. Anything they turn up will be added as a clearly-labelled *supplement* below this register, dated, with the register above left untouched. Nothing already frozen gets removed or edited. That keeps the pre-registration auditable: a later reader can see exactly what was known before any outcome was consulted, and exactly what arrived afterwards.
