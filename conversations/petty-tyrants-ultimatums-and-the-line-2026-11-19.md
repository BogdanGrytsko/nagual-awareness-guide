## What levers exist between adults

- **An ultimatum works only if it's believed**, and the other person knows you best. If it can't be
  carried out, it works against the one who gives it.
- **The consequences people actually use,** from mildest to most extreme: natural consequences (stop
  absorbing the cost); rebalancing chores; money you control; a third party; a ladder that ends in
  separation. That's roughly all of it. Between adults there's influence, not authority.
- **Salaries** exist because employment has a continuous reward and a credible exit. A marriage has
  neither in clean form. ("Ongoing… I see why they invented salaries.")
- **One-off rewards** stop the behaviour when they're paid out. That's human, not personal.
- **`yar`'s data point:** people don't change at a partner's request, and often not even when life hits
  them. Paying apprentices don't either. Some won't change. What's left is accept, change what's yours
  to change, or make it a dealbreaker and mean it.
- **"The decision is instant; sticking to it is another topic."** A real decision shows itself by holding
  when it's inconvenient.
